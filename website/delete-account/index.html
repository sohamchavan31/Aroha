Delete your Aroha account

Aroha by SOHREX Labs

From the app (instant)

  1. Open Aroha and tap your avatar to open your Profile.
  2. Go to Settings → Your data.
  3. Optional: tap Export my data first to keep a copy.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm with your password.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

If you can't open the app any more, email us from the address you signed up with. We will confirm the request and delete the account within 7 days.

Email a deletion request

What gets deleted

  • Your account: name, email and password.
  • Your profile: body stats, goals and targets.
  • Everything you logged: meals and custom foods, workouts and sets, water, sleep, weight check-ins, habits, tasks and missions.
  • Your Evolution Points, stage and streak.

Nothing is kept on our live database. Encrypted server backups age out within 30 days. We don't keep any of your data after that.
